Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Overview

PageVox is a document scanner and text-to-speech reader. We believe your documents are nobody else's business.

No Data Collection

PageVox does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. We do not run accounts, servers, analytics, or advertising, and we do not track you.

On-Device Processing

All document scanning, text recognition (OCR), and speech synthesis happen locally on your device. Your photos, files, and recognized text never leave your iPhone.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple, not PageVox, handles your payment information. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Children

PageVox does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it collects no data at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
